04 / Usage

Give it room.
Keep it recognizable.

Use the full logo at first introduction. Preserve the original proportions, colors, and transparent background. Place it on a calm, high-contrast surface.

Clear space

Reserve at least half the wordmark’s capital-letter height around all sides. Keep navigation, text, and borders outside that space.

A simpler mark for tiny spaces

Below 48 px, use the roof-and-lightning micro mark. The detailed Zeus face is reserved for larger placements.

Icon SVGIcon PNG

Minimum digital sizes

Horizontal: 180 px wide.
Stacked: 200 px wide.
Emblem: 48 px; 80 px preferred.
Micro mark: 16 px.

Keep the artwork intact

Do not stretch, tilt, recolor, add bevels or shadows, or place on busy photos. Use the correct light- or dark-background version.

Practical file choices

WebP for fast website headers. PNG for presentations and social graphics. SVG for layout and outlined lettering. The full-logo SVGs embed the raster emblem; they are not fully vector artwork.

05 / Brand direction

Authority with
a human purpose.

Speak plainly to homeowners and contractors. Be reassuring, specific, and useful. Let the Zeus identity provide personality while the service earns trust.

Protective

The roofline anchors the mythology in a real home and a practical need.

Decisive

The lightning bolt signals action. Use direct language and clear next steps.

Distinctive

The blue eyes are the recognizable signature. Keep the rest of the visual system restrained.

This kit establishes the visual identity. Lead-generation messaging and the public-site redesign are future work. Current website content and the separate app experience retain their existing behavior.
