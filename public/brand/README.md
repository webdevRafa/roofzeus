# Roof Zeus brand kit v4 — September 2026

Open `board.html` for the compact brand board or `index.html` for the full guide. `brand-board.png` is a ready-to-share overview.

## Direction

Zeus is shown as a compact head portrait with hair and beard. There is no chest, shoulder anatomy, or torso. The portrait sits directly above the roof. Blue eyes, ivory and slate facial planes, gold accents, and symmetric roof geometry carry the identity.

## Assets

- `roof-zeus-emblem.png`: 1,254 × 1,254 transparent master.
- `roof-zeus-logo-dark/light.svg/png/webp`: horizontal full logos. Dark/light names describe the intended background. PNG 2,000 × 480; WebP 800 × 192.
- `roof-zeus-stacked-dark/light.svg/png`: stacked logos. PNG 1,920 × 1,680.
- `roof-zeus-wordmark-dark/light.svg/png`: outlined wordmarks. PNG 1,760 × 300.
- `roof-zeus-icon.svg/png`: simplified three-gable roof micro mark. PNG 192 × 192.
- Website images show the identity in the existing public-site layout.

Logo SVGs have outlined lettering and an embedded raster emblem; they are not fully vector illustrations. Wordmark-only SVGs and the micro icon are true vector. No fonts are required to display these assets. The outlined wordmark is based on Georgia Bold. Current site UI typography remains Manrope and Poppins.

## Creation

Created with the built-in image-generation tool. All full-logo variants use the same head-only emblem with deterministic outlined lettering. Transparency and desktop/mobile presentation were checked.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a NEW original Roof Zeus logo emblem. Use case: logo-brand. ONLY a compact Zeus HEAD portrait with hair and beard, at most a tiny sliver of neck. Absolutely NO chest, NO shoulders, NO collarbones, NO muscles, NO torso, NO bust. A long handsome angular classical face looking slightly upward to the right in three-quarter view; vivid narrow electric cyan eyes with tiny white centers; proud brow, strong nose, elegant compact flowing ivory beard. Swept-back ivory hair with gold accents frames the head and stops at beard level. Flat premium graphic illustration with broad solid ivory planes, navy negative space and one slate shadow tone. Hair must not spread into a shoulder-like silhouette. Two slim gold lightning accents tucked behind the sides of the head and contained within its height. Directly beneath the HEAD is a separate, broad symmetric roofing symbol: one prominent central front gable and two identical smaller side gables, straight even roof pitches, gold outer fascia, ivory inner fascia, navy gable face and a centered four-pane gold window. All three roofs terminate at one straight level baseline, matching left/right eaves and clean balanced edges. The head is only about 55 percent the width of the roof assembly. The beard meets the roof ridge without any body between head and roof. Strong compact composition. Color palette: navy #081827, ivory #F5F1E8, gold #D6AC58, slate #6F8296, electric blue #43C8FF. Mostly flat sculptural style, clean large shapes, no gradients, no metallic finish, no 3D bevels, no photorealism. Small even margins, square canvas. Transparent PNG background with actual alpha transparency. NO TEXT, NO slogan, no mockup. Deliver ONE finished emblem.
