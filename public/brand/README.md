# Roof Zeus brand kit — September 2026

Open `index.html` for the visual brand guide, palette, typography, usage rules, and downloads. All assets work offline when this folder stays together.

## Files

- `roof-zeus-emblem.png`: original 1,254 × 1,254 transparent illustrated master.
- `roof-zeus-logo-dark.*` / `roof-zeus-logo-light.*`: horizontal full logo. Dark/light indicates the intended background. PNG: 2,000 × 480. Website WebP: 800 × 192.
- `roof-zeus-stacked-dark.*` / `roof-zeus-stacked-light.*`: stacked full logo. PNG: 1,920 × 2,000.
- `roof-zeus-wordmark-dark.*` / `roof-zeus-wordmark-light.*`: standalone outlined lettering. PNG: 1,760 × 300.
- `roof-zeus-icon.svg` / `.png`: simplified roof-and-lightning micro mark for browser icons. PNG: 192 × 192.

The logo SVGs contain outlined Georgia Bold lettering and the original embedded PNG emblem. They require no fonts or external image files, but the emblem remains raster artwork. Wordmark-only SVGs and the micro mark are true vector assets. Do not describe the full logos as fully vector masters. For large-format printing, commission a manual vector redraw of the approved emblem; do not enlarge it indefinitely.

Digital colors: Navy `#081827`, Gold `#D6AC58`, Ivory `#F5F1E8`, Blue `#43C8FF`. Shading in the generated illustration includes other tones. The current site's existing colors and copy are retained; the kit is the direction for future brand work.

## Website integration

Public marketing navigation, mobile menu, footer, and favicon use the new identity. App assets, authentication pages, organization logos, reports, routes, and workflows are unchanged. The guide is served at `/brand/index.html` and is marked `noindex`.

## Production notes

Emblem created with the built-in image-generation tool. Final lockups use the exact same emblem with deterministic outlined lettering and transparent exports. No source font files are distributed.

Emblem prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one exceptional original Roof Zeus emblem ONLY, no text. Premium trustworthy residential roofing connection brand. A noble Zeus head facing slightly right, unmistakable strong classical face, elegant flowing white hair and beard simplified into bold sculptural shapes, two small electric ice-blue eyes; integrate a single clean gold roof peak at the base and one elegant lightning bolt on left into a unified compact silhouette. Navy #081827, warm gold #D6AC58, ivory #F5F1E8, blue #43C8FF. Flat vector-like graphic with restrained two-tone shading, thick clean shapes and superb negative space. Refined boutique brand identity, authoritative and approachable, not angry esports mascot. Avoid metallic bevels, photorealism, gradients, glows, tiny hair strands, intricate windows, clip art, mockups. Square canvas, emblem filling 85 percent of canvas, centered. GENUINELY TRANSPARENT background with alpha, no checkerboard drawn into image. User reference inspiration: Zeus, roofing, gold/navy and blue eyes, but design a fresh simpler composition. Deliver only the emblem artwork.
