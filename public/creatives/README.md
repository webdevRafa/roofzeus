# RoofZeus — proposed campaign kit

September 23, 2026. Owned and operated by Devnetiks LLC.

These are original, pre-launch concepts for partner review, not campaigns with proven performance. Do not run paid traffic until lead delivery, buyer-approved consent, buying areas, traffic rules, and test acceptance are confirmed.

## Files

Each concept has a 1080×1080 square, 1080×1350 feed, and 1080×1920 vertical PNG, plus editable SVG artwork. The vertical version leaves room above and below the main content for placement controls; confirm crops and overlays in the ad platform before publishing. WebP files are lighter preview images.

The approved v8 RoofZeus logo is used unchanged. Roof photographs are illustrative, including AI-generated imagery; they are not customer projects, testimonials, storm reports, or proof of a roof's condition. RoofZeus is a referral service, not a roofing contractor.
