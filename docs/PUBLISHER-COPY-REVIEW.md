# RoofZeus publisher copy review — September 13, 2026

RoofZeus is owned and operated by Devnetiks LLC. The existing support@roofzeus.com mailbox is the public service, privacy, and withdrawal contact. This review improves consistency and disclosures; it is not Modernize approval or a legal certification. Live delivery remains behind its existing approval gates.

## Changes

- Readable footer ownership, support contact, referral compensation disclosure, and a direct privacy-choices link.
- Terms and Privacy identify the operator and remove retired contractor-registration offers. A receipt is distinguished from partner acceptance and a confirmed estimate.
- Live contact permission is shown in a 14px, high-contrast block beside the final action, with an unchecked required checkbox. The configured consent wording, advertiser tag, version, and exact server-side Post mapping are preserved.
- Live entry and first-step notices explain that TrustedForm records form entries before submission. Preview/demo copy states that no estimate request is sent, and never fabricates marketing permission.
- The privacy policy describes collected data, referral sharing, recording, browser ZIP storage, location tools, retention considerations, and how to request corrections, deletion, or withdrawal.
- Modernize's old /privacy-policy link returned a 404 in browser review. Its current footer links /privacy and /terms; both destinations were opened and verified. Public links now use those destinations.

## Sources and confirmed limits

[Modernize Ping/Post v3](https://apidoc.modernize.com/publishers/ping-post.html#tcpa-consent) requires the complete displayed consent language in homePhoneConsentLanguage and directs publishers to their account manager for the wording it accepts. Its current published contract requires TrustedForm and lists Jornaya as optional; confirm account-specific requirements. The public API documentation is marked draft and is not a substitute for the approved account contract.

[Modernize's Privacy Notice](https://modernize.com/privacy) (July 1, 2026) describes its own collection, sharing, and privacy choices. RoofZeus does not copy those broader practices into its policy or claim that they all run on this site. [Modernize's Terms](https://modernize.com/terms) govern its hosted site.

[ActiveProspect's TrustedForm Privacy Notice](https://activeprospect.com/trustedform-privacy-notice/) describes collection of form entries and session/browser information, including abandoned interactions, and vendor retention practices. These disclosures inform the recording explanation; a certificate is evidence, not permission by itself.
